## 2026-08-16 — REJECTED this scan (do not re-surface as new)
See `opportunities/2026-08-16-00-rejected.md` for full reasoning and sources.

- **AEMP / sterile-processing software** — incumbents already installed in CH hospitals
  (NEXUS at Spitäler Schaffhausen, USZ rebuilt its AEMP). Distribution gap unbridgeable solo.
- **Build an AI scribe / ambient documentation tool** — crowded (Ordicall, aaron.ai, 321med,
  Doctolib, medatixx), price already settled at €49–199/mo, and EU AI Act classifies medical
  AI as high-risk phasing in from August 2026. *Implementing* these survives as opportunity #3.
- **Productise the trading bot** — FINMA authorisation risk once it advises others for money.
  The exact line between education and regulated advice was NOT sourced; needs a lawyer.
  Keep the bot as a personal tool + portfolio piece for medtech interviews.
- **Nursing staff leasing** — licensed activity (Personalverleih), capital-heavy. Parked,
  unresearched.
- **Generic fitness / shift-worker content** — no unfair advantage, saturated. Screened out
  at Scout stage.
