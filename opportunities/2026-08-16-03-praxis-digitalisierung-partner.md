# Opportunity #3 — Practice Digitalisation Implementation Partner (Ostschweiz)

Date found: 2026-08-16
Status: New
Fit: Local (Rheineck/Ostschweiz), German-language, healthcare-adjacent, service
business with no product to build

## What it is

Swiss medical practices are behind on digitalisation and the bottleneck is not
software availability — it is that someone has to actually select, connect and
roll it out. Sell that hands-on implementation locally. You resell and implement
existing vendors' tools rather than building anything.

## EVIDENCE (sourced)

- **Swiss medical practices are considered digitally underdeveloped in European
  comparison**, with many practice information systems rated poorly by doctors. — [MediCloud Med](https://medicloudmed.ch/home/arztpraxis-digitalisierung-checkliste-2026/)
- Many practice systems are rated as difficult to use, outdated, or poorly
  integrable; many practices run isolated solutions with no interfaces to lab,
  radiology or billing, so data is transferred by hand and error sources
  multiply. — [MediCloud Med](https://medicloudmed.ch/home/praxisentwicklung-digitalisierung-bessere-versorgung/)
- Software selection is often made too late or on the wrong criteria — price or a
  colleague's recommendation decides, where future-proofing, integration
  capability and technical support should weigh equally. **That is a description
  of an advisory gap.** — [MediCloud Med](https://medicloudmed.ch/home/arztpraxis-digitalisierung-checkliste-2026/)
- A competitive Swiss practice-software market already exists to implement on top
  of (Tomedo, Vitodata et al.). — [avenios](https://avenios.ch/ratgeber/praxissoftware-vergleich)
- Recommended entry point is online appointment scheduling: manageable effort,
  immediately measurable benefit, high team acceptance — then expand stepwise.
  That is effectively a pre-validated first engagement. — [MediCloud Med](https://medicloudmed.ch/home/praxisentwicklung-digitalisierung-bessere-versorgung/)
- Adjacent tooling is mature and priced for small practices: DACH entry pricing
  for practice AI/voice tooling runs €49–199/month depending on practice size. — [Ordicall](https://www.ordicall.ai/blog/ki-telefonassistent-arztpraxis-vergleich-2026)

## VALIDATOR — Demand evidence: **Some**

The *problem* is well sourced and repeatedly stated by Swiss industry sources.
What is not sourced is that practices will pay an independent implementer rather
than lean on their software vendor's own support. That is the open question, and
it is cheap to answer — there are practices within driving distance.

Note also that the sources documenting this problem are themselves vendors and
service providers in the space, so they have an interest in describing the gap as
large. Discount accordingly.

# RE-SCAN ADDENDUM — 2026-08-16, second run

**The customer here is right. The way in was wrong.**

The second scan found that the same practices carry a *legally mandatory, inspected*
obligation around medical-device reprocessing — and that this is a subject where an OR
professional has genuine expertise, rather than the generic IT-implementer expertise
this opportunity originally assumed.

See `opportunities/2026-08-16-06-mepv-reprocessing-compliance.md` for the full sourced
case. In short: reprocessing must follow validated procedures under MepV, practices are
inspected by the **cantonal pharmacist** against a published FMH/KIGAP checklist, and
consulting an external hygiene expert is already a recommended route for new practices.
— [Swissmedic](https://www.swissmedic.ch/swissmedic/en/home/medical-devices/reprocessing---maintenance/reprocessing.html), [FMH checklist](https://www.guidelines.fmh.ch/index_detail.cfm?id=15EF7A1D403B46A9FE7E3B77D60AC05E), [Swiss Cleanroom Community](https://www.swisscleanroomcommunity.ch/de/scc-community/community/blog?news=558)

## What to change

Keep this opportunity, keep the target list, but **lead with compliance, not
digitalisation.** Concretely:

- Compliance is a sharper opening than efficiency. "Are you ready for your next
  inspection?" gets a meeting; "is your admin inefficient?" gets a polite no.
- Reprocessing is where you have standing. Practice IT is where you have none — and
  the sources describing the digitalisation gap are themselves vendors selling into it.
- Digitalisation then becomes the **second** sale to an existing client who already
  trusts you, which is a far easier sale than the first one.

The five discovery calls in the checklist below are still exactly right — just change
the question. Instead of asking only what is still retyped by hand, ask about the last
inspection too. One call, both answers.

**Score unchanged at 6.8.** The re-scan changed the entry strategy, not the economics,
and it would be dishonest to inflate the score for a plan that has still never been
tested on a real practice.

## STRATEGIST — plan

- **Offer:** A fixed-price "digitalisation starter" for a single practice — online
  booking live, plus one integration that removes manual re-typing.
- **Target customer:** Small independent practices (1–3 doctors) in Ostschweiz.
- **Revenue:** Fixed project fee per practice, optional monthly support retainer.
  Possibly vendor referral commission, which must be disclosed to the client.
- **Cost/effort to start:** Low — a laptop, and time. No inventory, no product.
- **Biggest risk:** Sales cycle. Doctors are hard to reach and slow to commit, and
  this needs in-person selling. Secondary risk: taking responsibility for patient
  data handling brings real compliance obligations, including the EU AI Act phasing
  in from August 2026 for anything AI-touching. — [docreport.eu](https://docreport.eu/blog/dsgvo-arztpraxis-software-datenschutz-leitfaden-ki)

## BUILDER — first steps

1. List every independent practice within 30 minutes of Rheineck.
2. Call five and ask one question: "what part of your admin is still on paper or
   retyped by hand?" Do not sell. Log the answers.
3. If a pattern shows up, learn one booking tool and one PVS well enough to
   install them.
4. Do the first practice at cost, in exchange for a written reference.
5. Price the second one properly.
6. **Kill criteria:** if five calls produce no practice willing to book a free
   look at their setup, the sales cycle is the wall — stop here.
