# Opportunity #4 — Freelance Medical Writing / MDR Regulatory Documentation

Date found: 2026-08-16
Status: New
Fit: Clinical background + German language; slow-burn, high-rate second income

## What it is

Medical device manufacturers must produce large volumes of regulatory and
clinical documentation under EU MDR. This work is billed hourly by freelancers and
can be done remotely and part-time — which fits a second income around shift work
better than most options in this scan.

## EVIDENCE (sourced)

- Average freelance hourly rate for medical writing in the German-speaking market:
  **€88/hour**. For MDR-specific expertise: **€110/hour**. — [freelancermap.de — Medical Writer](https://www.freelancermap.de/freelancer-verzeichnis/medical-writer-1844), [freelancermap.de — MDR](https://www.freelancermap.de/freelancer-verzeichnis/mdr-medical-device-regulation-46796)
- International benchmark: regulatory writers charge from USD 50/hour for basic
  work and USD 100+/hour for complex projects involving deeper consulting. — [Kolabtree](https://www.kolabtree.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-hire-a-freelance-medical-writer/)
- Demand exists in Switzerland specifically — 38 medical-writing positions listed
  on LinkedIn CH. — [LinkedIn CH](https://ch.linkedin.com/jobs/medical-writing-stellen)
- Established freelancer marketplaces already broker this work in the DACH region. — [freelance.de](https://www.freelance.de/Medical-writing-Freelancer), [regulatory-affairs-manager.com](https://www.regulatory-affairs-manager.com/marktplatz/medical-writing/)
- The required knowledge base is explicit and learnable: MDR, approval procedures,
  ISO 13485 (quality management) and ISO 14971 (risk management); structured
  continuing-education programmes for exactly this combination exist. — [jobvector](https://www.jobvector.de/job/weiterbildung-ef132631a5524964/)

## MY READ (assumption — not sourced)

- Clinical experience helps credibility but is **not** a substitute for regulatory
  qualification. This is the assumption that most affects the score: I am treating
  the entry barrier as real and high, not as a formality.
- The €88–110/hour figures are advertised rates on freelancer directories, which
  systematically overstate what a newcomer with no portfolio actually gets. Treat
  as a ceiling, not a starting rate.

## VALIDATOR — Demand evidence: **Some to Strong**

Rates and job listings are directly observable, which is real evidence. But every
sourced data point describes the market for *qualified* writers. I found no
evidence about demand for entrants without regulatory credentials — which is the
actual starting position here. Demand for the finished profile: strong. Demand for
the current profile: unevidenced.

## STRATEGIST — plan

- **Offer:** Regulatory/clinical documentation support to medtech manufacturers,
  eventually specialising in surgical device documentation where OR experience is
  a genuine edge.
- **Target customer:** Small and mid-size device manufacturers without in-house
  regulatory teams; also agencies subcontracting overflow.
- **Revenue:** Hourly or per-document, remote, schedulable around shifts.
- **Cost/effort to start:** Real money and months of study for MDR/ISO training —
  the only option in this scan with a meaningful cash barrier to entry.
- **Biggest risk:** Paying for training and then failing to win a first contract.
  The qualification does not sell itself.

## BUILDER — first steps

1. Before spending anything on training: message 5 medical writers on
   [freelancermap.de](https://www.freelancermap.de/freelancer-verzeichnis/medical-writer-1844) and ask how they got their first contract and whether
   clinical background helped. This costs nothing and de-risks the whole path.
2. Price out one MDR/ISO 13485 course. Get a real number, not an estimate.
3. Compare that number and timeline against Opportunity #2, which requires no
   investment at all. They compete for the same hours.
4. If proceeding: take the course, then build one sample document (a public-domain
   device write-up) as a portfolio piece.
5. Register on the DACH freelancer marketplaces and quote below the €88 average
   for the first three contracts to build a track record.
6. **Kill criteria:** if step 1 shows first contracts consistently come through
   prior in-industry employment, do Opportunity #2 first and revisit this later —
   the two are sequential, not competing.
